## Tips to Win More Rounds After the Spelling Race Update

Whether you are chasing a personal best or just trying to keep up with friends, a handful of habits separate consistent winners from everyone else.

- **Read the whole prompt before typing.** Skipping ahead is the most common cause of avoidable mistakes.
- **Prioritize accuracy, then speed.** A wrong answer earns nothing, so a fast miss is worse than a slightly slower hit.
- **Play in short sessions.** Three or four focused matches beat a tired thirty-minute grind.
- **Use private servers to warm up.** Low-pressure rounds with friends help you build rhythm before jumping into public matches.
- **Learn the word pool.** Repeated exposure to the same categories makes recognition nearly automatic over time.
- **Keep your setup clean.** A stable connection and a distraction-free screen matter more than most players admit.

Here is how those tips translate into practical priorities:

| Priority | What to do | Expected payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Highest | Answer accurately before answering fast | Fewer wasted rounds |
| High | Play short, focused sessions | Better block-per-minute rate |
| Medium | Warm up in a private server | Smoother transition to public play |
| Medium | Learn common word patterns | Faster recognition over time |
| Low | Optimize your hardware | Marginal but real gains |

Community reports consistently suggest that the biggest jump in performance comes from the first two rows. Everything else is fine-tuning.
